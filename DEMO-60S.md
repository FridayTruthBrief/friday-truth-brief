# Friday Truth Brief — 60-second demo script

Screen-recording guide for a ~60s walkthrough. Record at phone width (~390px) or desktop; dark mode first.

**CTA placeholder:** `https://YOUR-DEMO-URL/` (or “local demo — ask for link”)

---

## Timing overview

| Time | Screen | Action |
|------|--------|--------|
| 0:00–0:08 | Home (dark) | Show Top 10 list + banner |
| 0:08–0:14 | Home | Toggle light mode, pause, toggle back to dark |
| 0:14–0:28 | Story detail | Open rank #1 (or any story) |
| 0:28–0:42 | Story sections | Scroll: Known facts → Misrepresentations → Narratives |
| 0:42–0:52 | Plans | Scroll home Plans (or jump via #plans) |
| 0:52–0:60 | CTA card | End card / voiceover CTA |

## Shot-by-shot

### 0:00–0:08 — Home, dark mode
- **Show:** Homepage with demo banner, week chip, Top 10 cards.
- **Do:** Slow scroll of first 2–3 story cards.
- **Voiceover:** “Friday Truth Brief — a weekly, evidence-first Top 10. Not affiliated with X; inspired by a clean reader UX.”
- **Caption (optional):** `Friday Truth Brief · sample edition`

### 0:08–0:14 — Theme toggle
- **Show:** Tap sun/moon control in the header.
- **Do:** Switch to light, hold ~2s, switch back to dark.
- **Voiceover:** “Light and dark — same calm layout.”
- **Caption:** `Theme toggle`

### 0:14–0:28 — Open a story
- **Show:** Tap story #1 (or highest-ranked visible card).
- **Do:** Land on story hero: rank, title, summary, Overview.
- **Voiceover:** “Open any story for overview, then the three layers that matter.”
- **Caption:** `Story detail`

### 0:28–0:42 — Facts / misrep / narratives
- **Show:** Scroll through sections in order.
- **Do:** Pause briefly on:
  1. **Known facts** (claim + evidence + source links)
  2. **Misrepresentations** (outlet + severity chip)
  3. **Narratives & spin** (type badge + how it misleads)
- **Voiceover:** “Known facts with citations. Misrepresentations named. Narrative techniques called out—so you see the record and the spin.”
- **Captions:** `Known facts` → `Misrepresentations` → `Narratives`

### 0:42–0:52 — Plans
- **Show:** Back to home (or scroll to Plans). Highlight Free / Plus / Global cards.
- **Do:** Hold on the three price cards + “concept only” note.
- **Voiceover:** “Access concept: free Top 10, about a dollar for Top 20, about three for global plus US and local. No checkout in this demo.”
- **Caption:** `Plans · concept`

### 0:52–0:60 — CTA
- **Show:** End frame: logo/title + one line + link placeholder.
- **On-screen text:**
  ```
  Friday Truth Brief
  Local demo · ask for a walkthrough
  https://YOUR-DEMO-URL/
  ```
- **Voiceover:** “Independent prototype by Cory Brewer. Link in the description—happy to walk you through.”
- **Caption:** `Not affiliated with X`

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Start in **dark** mode (`data-theme="dark"` or toggle once before record)
- [ ] Demo banner visible (honest: sample edition)
- [ ] Audio: one take, conversational, no hype words (“revolutionary,” etc.)
- [ ] End freeze 2–3 seconds on CTA so viewers can read the URL
- [ ] Export 1080p; 9:16 for X/Reels or 16:9 for email/deck
