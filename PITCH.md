# Friday Truth Brief — Pitch to X

**Independent prototype by Cory Brewer.** Inspired by X’s clean reader UX. **Not affiliated with X.** No claim of partnership, endorsement, or inside access.

---

## Problem

Political news moves fast and breaks trust faster. Readers get headlines, clips, and takes—often before they get a clear record of what is actually documented. Spin from every side fills the gap. People who want “just the facts” still have to dig through outlets, quote-tweets, and thread wars.

## Insight

The missing product isn’t another opinion feed. It’s a **weekly, ranked, evidence-first brief**: known facts with citations, explicit call-outs of misrepresentations, and a short map of narrative techniques—so readers can see the record and the spin in one place.

X already hosts the conversation. A calm Friday brief that sits *beside* the firehose—not inside it as ragebait—fits how serious users already use the platform: scan, verify, decide.

## Product

**Friday Truth Brief** — every Friday, Top political stories in a mobile-clean reader:

1. **Overview** — what happened, neutrally.
2. **Known facts** — claims + evidence + sources.
3. **Misrepresentations** — outlet, claim made, why inaccurate, severity.
4. **Narratives & spin** — framing patterns (headline, selective emphasis, etc.) and how they mislead.

Local static demo exists today (sample edition for layout). Theme toggle, story detail, plans mock, PWA shell ready for HTTPS hosting.

## Why X

- X is where political narrative forms in public.
- Clean reading UX and distribution are already native strengths.
- A weekly “truth brief” product reinforces X as the place for *signal*, not only hot takes.
- Format can live as a standalone web/PWA experience, an X-native surface, or a licensed module—without pretending this pitch is an official X product.

## Monetization

Simple, non-annoying tiers (concept):

| Tier | Price | Access |
|------|-------|--------|
| Free | $0 | Top 10 weekly |
| Plus | ~$1 / mo | Top 20 weekly |
| Global + local | ~$3 / mo | Global Top 10 + US + local/county layers |

Optional: **one labeled sponsored slot** in the feed—never popups, never interruptive ads. Subscriptions carry the product; ads stay tasteful or absent.

## Proof

- Working **local demo** with full story detail UI (facts / misrep / narratives).
- X-inspired light/dark theme; Plans mock on the homepage.
- PWA manifest + service worker coded for install-once-hosted (public HTTPS).

This is a prototype to evaluate fit—not a live newsroom or App Store listing.

## Ask

Any one of:

1. **Small pilot** — format + distribution experiment with a real Friday edition.
2. **License the format** — X (or a partner) runs the brief under clear branding rules.
3. **Build-with** — scoped collaboration to ship a first real week and measure retention.

Open to the path that respects independence until a deal exists.

## What this is not

- Not an official X product or endorsed by X Corp.
- Not a live fact-check network or newsroom (demo data is synthetic).
- Not an App Store / Play Store native app (installable PWA after HTTPS hosting).
- Not partisan media dressed as “truth.”
- Not a request for credentials, data access, or special API privileges in this pitch.
